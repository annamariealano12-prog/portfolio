    About Me

    Engineer by training, detail-checker by habit

    I’m a Computer Engineering graduate from Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng San Pablo, with coursework in hardware, software, and networking. That mix taught me to look at how a whole system fits together, not just one part of it.

    Since 2024, I’ve worked as a Content Creator at Mathspace, editing and refining student worksheets for accuracy, clarity, consistency, and formatting. My work also includes data annotation and using large language models (LLMs) to help with writing, editing, and reviewing. I let AI speed up the routine parts, and I check the results by hand.

    I’m most interested in AI-assisted workflows, educational content, quality assurance, and small web tools that save time.
